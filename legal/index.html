Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 10th September 2026

Crumb respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is stored, how it is used, and how website analytics are handled.

What We Store

  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord user IDs where required for bot functionality
  • Discord channel IDs where required for configuration
  • Bot configuration and server settings
  • Moderation-related data such as warnings where applicable

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as real names, passwords, payment information, or the contents of private messages.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to provide and operate Crumb's features, including moderation, server configuration, utilities, reminders, and other bot functionality.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics only if you give consent through the analytics banner displayed on this website.

If you consent, Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, session duration, device type, browser type, and general usage patterns.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies. This information is used to understand website usage and improve the Crumb website.

If you decline analytics, Google Analytics will not be enabled through this website.

You can change your analytics preference at any time using the Analytics Settings button.

You can view Google's privacy policy here: Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your stored bot data with third parties except where required by law or where necessary to operate third-party services used by Crumb.

Data Removal

You can request deletion of data associated with your server or Discord account by contacting us through the Crumb community server.

  • Remove Crumb from your Discord server
  • Contact the developers through the community server
  • Use an available data-deletion command where provided
